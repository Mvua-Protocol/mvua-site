How Mvua works

The idea, the problem, the machine, and the life of a policy. Read this to build a working mental model before the reference fills in the details.

What parametric insurance is

Traditional insurance pays after someone assesses a loss. An adjuster visits, files paperwork, and the claim is disputed and settled. For a smallholder with a two hectare plot, that process costs more than the premium is worth, so traditional insurers simply do not offer the product at all.

Parametric insurance, also called index based insurance, removes the assessment. The contract pays when an objective, measurable index crosses an agreed threshold: cumulative rainfall below a fraction of its historical median, a run of consecutive dry days, a drop in satellite vegetation health. The data is the claim.

The trade off is basis risk: the index can disagree with any single farm's actual outcome. Rain may fall on the next field and miss yours. Mvua's mitigation is regional granularity, careful index design, and a severity curve rather than an all or nothing cliff, all documented openly. This is a real limitation of every parametric product, not a defect specific to Mvua.

The machine at a glance

Mvua is five Soroban contracts on Stellar plus a thin web layer. Separating concerns means a defect in one contract cannot silently corrupt another.

CAPITAL

risk-pool

Holds premiums and liquidity provider capital in USDC across a junior and a senior tranche. Moves a season through Open, Active, Closed, and Settled, and applies a loss waterfall where the junior tranche absorbs first loss.

TERMS

policy

Mints a certificate per purchase with its region, coverage window, and severity curve, and moves it through a fixed lifecycle. Holds no value itself.

DATA

oracle-adapter

Verifies signed weather observations and returns a trusted median over fresh, unchallenged data from at least two independent publishers.

DECISION

trigger-engine

Evaluates an index definition deterministically with integer only maths and produces a severity in basis points.

SETTLEMENT

payout-vault

Releases payouts as resumable batches into claimable balances, so a farmer never needs XLM to receive money. Makes no decisions of its own.

The two flows

Buying cover

  1. A buyer or a cooperative requests a quote for a region, a window, and a coverage amount. The price comes from the pool's premium curve.
  2. The purchase collects the premium into the risk pool and mints an Active certificate.
  3. A cooperative can fund a bounded batch of policies in one atomic call, each owned by its own farmer.

Getting paid

  1. Through the season, independent publishers submit signed daily rainfall readings for the region.
  2. The adapter builds a trusted median, with a challenge window for guardians to exclude a bad reading.
  3. At window end the trigger engine computes a severity in basis points against the policy's index definition.
  4. If the index qualifies, the payout vault releases each payout into a claimable balance. No claim is filed and no adjuster is involved.

The life of a policy

A policy moves through defined states, all verifiable on chain.

StateWhat it means
ActiveThe premium is paid and the certificate is minted with its region, window, and severity curve. Publishers deliver observations and the trigger engine evaluates the index.
TriggeredThe index crossed its threshold and the trigger was finalized after the challenge window.
PaidThe payout vault settled the payout into a claimable balance for the holder.
ExpiredThe coverage window ended without a trigger.
CancelledCancelled before the coverage window opened, with a refund less a fee.

What stays trusted

Every money decision is a function of public inputs: the signed rainfall readings, the index definition, and the policy terms. Two things are still trusted, and the design names them rather than hiding them. Guardians hold the ability to challenge readings, pause, and propose publishers, bounded by a timelock and a cancellation window. And the data sources themselves, which the median and the challenge window reduce reliance on but cannot make infallible.
