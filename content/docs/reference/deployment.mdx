Mvua runs on Stellar testnet today. These are the deployed contract addresses and a link to the first full season that settled on chain. This is pre alpha: the addresses are provisional, they can change on a redeploy and nothing here holds real funds.

## Network

Stellar testnet. Settlement is in a test USDC style token (a Stellar Asset Contract), not mainnet USDC.

## Contracts

Each contract has one job (see the [protocol reference](/docs/reference/protocol)). Open any address on the explorer to read its live state.

The `oracle-adapter` was redeployed on 2026-11-09 with a fix that accepts each signed reading only once and counts one value per publisher. The first season below ran on the previous oracle ([CD4XMGP7...2PLWI](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/contract/CD4XMGP7ZSJW7X4HDSTVJAVV4ZXYWVXFSIMM24FUTIT6L3AK2IN2PLWI)), and its transactions remain valid.

## The first settled season

A full season ran end to end against real wall-clock time: a farmer bought cover, independent publishers submitted signed rainfall, the index triggered on a drought, the trigger finalized after the 24 hour challenge window and the payout was released and claimed. The numbers matched the golden test vector exactly, a severity of 2857 basis points, paid as 2857000 stroops on the policy.

- [Finalize transaction](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/81d77cc957f6b54741b9a713bb4e3f232edb5ccb2da7bfba3d824b60b9678d1c): locks the severity at 2857 bps after the challenge window.
- [Payout transaction](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/f3dc31b40ecea54034489099959d59a6b859d158f1f2c081a3cf2a5da18bd087): the vault records the 2857000 payout.
- [Claim transaction](https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/tx/54dbde6b4f543cb3db7b0d1544b9ee09f857d3d27fa6223fa4f4ea4fbef44e3e): the farmer receives the payout.
